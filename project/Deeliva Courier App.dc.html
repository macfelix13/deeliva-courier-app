Brief · read me

What I assumed

Same-day parcel courier, one city, two apps in one shell: a sender flow (send → track → history) and a courier flow (accept → pickup → scan → proof). Copy is plain and friendly; every number is illustrative.

Design reasoning

Industry is a wireframe system, so the app reads as a technical instrument: hairline blueprint frames with registration marks, condensed headings, one steel accent, and the deep accent field reserved for the two moments that matter — the live shipment and a completed delivery. No filled cards, no second colour.

Variations live in Tweaks

The four axes you picked are switches, not separate mockups — home layout, tracking treatment, checkout flow and navigation pattern. Flip them in the Tweaks panel and the whole prototype stays working.
